# Source-channel matrix

Use this matrix to plan later evidence acquisition; this Skill does not acquire it.
Record each planned route as exactly `public-available`, `public-partial`,
`client-required`, `proxy-available`, or `unknown`. Prefer sources in this order:
regulators/government/exchanges/courts; company filings; associations/standards/
procurement; patents/certifications/products/channels; credible research/databases/
media; then directional job/review/traffic/social sources. Directional sources are
leads, not proof.

| Domain | Primary public sources | Directional sources | Likely client-only records | Allowed substitutes | Decision risk if missing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| identity | registries, exchanges, regulator filings, official site | directories, media | incorporation records, ownership chart | official identity source, `[A]` | wrong entity/jurisdiction |
| strategy | annual reports, investor presentations, government plans | executive interviews, news | board plans, strategy papers | public statements, `[A]` | wrong strategic intent |
| market | government statistics, associations, standards, procurement | research/media, traffic/social | commissioned research, sell-out data | credible market study, proxy | market size or priority unsupported |
| customers | consumer agencies, public tenders | reviews, social, web traffic | CRM, surveys, churn/cohorts | approved interviews, proxy | demand/retention thesis unverified |
| product/pricing | catalogs, filings, product certifications | price trackers, reviews | price lists, discounts, transactions | published offers, proxy | price/value case unreliable |
| competition | filings, regulator/exchange disclosures, patents | media, jobs, reviews, traffic | win/loss, account intelligence | sourced competitor profiles | response misdirected |
| revenue/profit | filings, exchange reports, audited accounts | earnings coverage | management accounts, revenue bridge | public financials, proxy | financial-health report incomplete |
| cost | filings, procurement awards, standards | wage/commodity benchmarks | GL, supplier spend, BOM | benchmarks explicitly labeled `[E]` | savings case speculative |
| cash/debt | filings, court records, credit disclosures | debt-market coverage | bank balances, facilities, covenants | audited statements, proxy | liquidity/default exposure missed |
| working capital | filings | sector benchmarks | AR/AP aging, inventory detail | public ratios, proxy | cash conversion misread |
| supply chain | customs/procurement, certifications, recalls | shipping/news, job posts | supplier terms, inventory, capacity | public logistics indicators | service/resilience risk unknown |
| operations | safety/regulator data, certifications | reviews, traffic | throughput, quality, process times | benchmarks, time study request | productivity case unsupported |
| organization/talent | filings, required disclosures | job posts, employer reviews | org chart, pay, engagement, attrition | public leadership/roles | feasibility and capacity unknown |
| digital/data | filings, data/security regulators, patents | engineering jobs, vendor claims | architecture, data inventory, usage logs | public stack signals, `[A]` | AI/digital value and risk unknown |
| legal/regulatory | regulators, government, courts, standards | legal media | permits, investigations, counsel advice | official texts, approved counsel | noncompliance or delay risk |
| transactions | exchange filings, merger control notices, courts | deal databases, media | IM, SPA, diligence room | public transaction comps | deal thesis incomplete |
| valuation | filings, exchange data, transaction disclosures | market databases, analyst commentary | forecasts, cap table, debt terms | trading/transaction comps, proxy | valuation range not defensible |

Never send private text, client records, or private-derived sensitive search terms to public services. Ask the client or reviewer to approve an anonymized, non-sensitive query or retain the route as `client-required`/`unknown`. A substitute never changes the underlying acquisition route without documenting the evidence gap and decision risk.
